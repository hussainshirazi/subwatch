## What Pockitry is
Pockitry helps you keep track of your subscriptions, bills and one-time purchases. Everything you enter stays on your phone, unless you turn on the optional sync, which keeps an encrypted copy in your own Google Drive (see the [privacy policy](privacy-policy)). It is a record-keeping tool: it doesn't move money, connect to your bank, cancel anything for you, or give financial advice.

## Free and Pro

- **Pockitry Pro** unlocks the features listed on the app's Pro screen. You can buy it in Google Play as a **monthly** or **yearly subscription**, or as a **lifetime** one-time purchase.
